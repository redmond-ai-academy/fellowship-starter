## The structure: Present, Past, Future, answer first

1. **Name and anchor.** "I am ___, a final-year ___ student at ___, Coimbatore."
2. **Answer first: what I want.** "I joined this Fellowship to ___."
3. **Past: one concrete example.** "For example, I built / fixed / learned ___, and it taught me ___."
4. **Present: where I am now.** "Right now I am strongest at ___ and I want to improve ___."
5. **Next step: the ask.** "This week I will ___. I would like help with ___."

Aim for about five sentences.
